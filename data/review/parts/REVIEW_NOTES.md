## 5. Other things to check

- **Services count as labour.** Lines such as 'Wheel alignment (service)', 'Diagnostic scan + basic checks (service)' and 'Recovery / towing service' are loaded as service charges (`fault_service_charges`), never as parts. Check that each is a real charge for that repair and is not already inside the sheet's labour figure.
- **Consumables** (coolant, brake fluid, oil, grease, refrigerant) are detected by name and shown separately in estimates. Check the split for parts such as 'Coolant hose clamp' (kept as a part).
- **File 9 fault links:** 185 of 185 approved; each part is named in the fault's own price text, and 129 also quote the part number.
- **Brands merged:** 'Hyundai Genuine (Mobis)' and 'Hyundai Genuine (via Motrparts)' -> 'Hyundai Genuine'; 'Amaron FLO 40B20L' -> 'Amaron FLO'; 'Maruti Genuine (via Motrparts)' -> 'Maruti Genuine'.
- **Labour** comes from the sheet's labour column per segment ('Delhi NCR metro estimate'). City rates are empty in `data/catalogue/zone_labour_rates.yaml` until the team fills them.
